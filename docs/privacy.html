Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 6, 2026 · App: BuildScript AI · Package: com.buildscript.ai

BuildScript AI ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: Email address and display name via Firebase Authentication.
  • Vehicle data: Vehicle details you enter (make, model, year, mileage, modifications).
  • Usage data: App interactions and AI chat history to improve our service.
  • Device information: Device type, OS, and app version for compatibility.
  • Location data: GPS location when you use Tow Services or Find Shops Near Me (only with your permission).
  • Purchase information: Subscription status managed through Google Play Billing and RevenueCat. We do not store payment card details.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the BuildScript AI service
  • To personalize your experience and remember your vehicles
  • To process subscriptions and restore purchases
  • To send important service updates
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication and cloud database
  • Google Gemini AI: AI-powered chat and diagnostics
  • RevenueCat: Subscription management
  • Google Play Billing: Payment processing
  • Google Maps: Nearby shop and tow service locations

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase Firestore with industry-standard encryption.

5. Location Data

Location permission is requested only when you use Tow Services or Find Shops Near Me. Location data is not stored on our servers.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion by contacting us. Data will be removed within 30 days of account deletion.

7. Children's Privacy

BuildScript AI is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time and will notify you of significant changes.
