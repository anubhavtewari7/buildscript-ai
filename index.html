Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

BuildScript AI ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the BuildScript AI mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: Name and email address when you create an account.
  • Vehicle data: Vehicle information and diagnostic codes you enter or scan.
  • Location data: Approximate or precise location when you use roadside assistance or nearby shop features (optional, with your permission).
  • Usage data: How you interact with the app, including features used and session duration.
  • Purchase history: Subscription and purchase records processed through Google Play.
  • Device identifiers: Device ID and app instance identifiers for analytics and functionality.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide AI-powered diagnostic and repair assistance
  • To manage your account and subscriptions
  • To improve app features and performance
  • To provide location-based services (tow trucks, nearby shops)
  • To send important service notifications

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication and data storage
  • Google Gemini: AI processing of diagnostic queries
  • RevenueCat: Subscription management
  • Google Play: Payment processing

4. Location Data

Location is only collected when you actively use the tow service or nearby shops features. It is used to find relevant services near you and is not stored permanently.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion at any time by contacting us.

6. Your Rights

7. Children's Privacy

8. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit and at rest to protect your data.
